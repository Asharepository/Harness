How to play

Move: ↑ ↓ ← → or W A S D
Pause: Space toggles pause without losing progress.
Restart: R or the restart buttons reset the run instantly.
Speed-up: every 5 foods increases the snake speed by one level.

Strategy notes

Build lanes. Leave yourself a return path instead of chasing every food directly.
Respect the ramp. Every fifth food shortens reaction time, so start wider turns before level two.
Keyboard-first. Click the board once to focus it, then play entirely from the keyboard with visible focus states.

Designed as a compact arcade surface: readable at phone size, calm at rest, sharp under pressure.
